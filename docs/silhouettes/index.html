The 40-pixel test

Every combatant in every roster, drawn from the real ring geometry at exactly 40 pixels tall in pure black — the size and the colour the player actually sees them at, behind haze, for a fraction of a second. If two shapes on this page are hard to tell apart at 1×, they are the same figure in the game. The wide strip above each grid stages the cast at its authored sizes and opacities, which is the separate question of whether the backdrop reads as depth.

Only 1× is the test. The others are for finding out why a shape failed it.
